Materials

A natural look and efficient use of wood.

We combine natural veneer on furniture panels with solid wood for elements and details. This keeps the natural texture and a premium look while using the timber efficiently.

Natural veneer allows a wide range of colours and lacquer finishes, matched to the specification of each project.

How we work

From drawing to finished series.

  1. 01

    Technical brief

    We receive drawings, specifications, references or information about an existing design.

  2. 02

    Production planning

    We confirm materials, dimensions, finishes, minimum quantities and technical requirements.

  3. 03

    Prototype or sample

    When the order requires it, we make a prototype or sample for review and approval.

  4. 04

    Approval and production

    Once approved, we produce the order to the agreed specification and schedule.

  5. 05

    Quality control

    We check every piece against the applicable requirements for dimensions, colour, finish and joints.

  6. 06

    Ready for shipping

    We prepare the goods for shipping — assembled or flat-packed — and pack them to the client's requirements.

Our markets

Made in Bulgaria. Proven internationally.

KAM manufactures furniture for clients in Europe and North America. We have worked for the US market since 2003, and for Germany and the Netherlands since 2006.

Our experience covers serial office furniture, furniture collections for brands, and furnishing for hotels and public buildings. Production takes place at our base in Vratsa, Bulgaria. Our equipment allows us to deliver large orders in short lead times.
